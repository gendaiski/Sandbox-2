God's Eye View Globe

The bundled world layers on a live globe, steered by the voice agent's own tools Upstream projectInstall and test code

God's Eye View itself draws a photorealistic Cesium globe fed by live public sources (flights, ships, satellites, quakes, cameras), which a page cannot reach. This globe draws the layers the repository bundles: Natural Earth borders, 712 TeleGeography submarine cables and 1,917 landing points, 4,351 OpenStreetMap datacenters, 704 dams and the Yale bright stars. Typed commands go to Claude with the voice agent's own tool definitions from server/providers/openai.

Tools this page gives Claude

Names, descriptions and parameters are GEV_REALTIME_TOOLS from server/providers/openai/tools.js, the definitions its realtime voice model receives. annotate_map and analyst_query are trimmed here to the parts this page implements.

flowchart LR

A voice turn

sequenceDiagram
  participant U as You
  participant V as Realtime model
  participant G as Globe
  U->>V: "How many datacenters are in Ireland?"
  V->>G: analyst_query(local-datacenters, region Ireland)
  G-->>V: count and names
  V->>G: fly_to_location(query Ireland)
