ChunkNorris Studio

Header-aware chunking of Markdown, HTML and PDF for retrieval Upstream projectRun its Streamlit app

An interactive model of ChunkNorris, not affiliated with wikit-ai. The MarkdownChunker algorithm is ported line for line (ToC tree, recursive section packing, hard-max newline splits that keep tables and lists whole, minimum-size filtering). Markdown, HTML and the sample PDF are parsed in your browser.

Pipeline


flowchart LR
  SRC["PDF, DOCX, HTML, Markdown,
XLSX, Jupyter"] --> P{"Parser"}
  P --> MD["MarkdownDoc
(lines, pages)"]
  MD --> TOC["ToC tree from headers
up to max_headers_to_use"]
  TOC --> PACK["Pack sections that fit
max_chunk_word_count"]
  PACK --> SPLIT["Split above hard max on newlines
(not inside tables, lists, code)"]
  SPLIT --> DROP["Drop chunks under
min_chunk_word_count"]
  DROP --> OUT["Chunks with header context
and page numbers"]
