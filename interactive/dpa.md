# Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum ("Addendum") forms part of the Master Services Agreement between Northwind Legal Technologies Ltd. ("Processor") and Contoso Holdings LLC ("Controller"). It applies whenever the Processor handles Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

## 1. Scope and Roles

### 1.1 Roles of the Parties

The Controller determines the purposes and means of processing. The Processor processes Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Controller, including with regard to transfers to a third country.

### 1.2 Categories of Data

The Processor will handle contact details of Controller staff, the names of counterparties appearing in contracts, and metadata describing those contracts. No special category data is processed under this Addendum.

## 2. Security Measures

The Processor will maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption of data at rest with AES-256, encryption in transit with TLS 1.2 or later, role-based access control, and quarterly access reviews.

Security incidents affecting Personal Data will be reported to the Controller without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of discovery.

## 3. Sub-processors

The Controller authorises the following sub-processors. The Processor will give thirty (30) days' notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor, during which the Controller may object on reasonable grounds.

| Sub-processor | Service | Location |
|---------------|---------|----------|
| Fabrikam Cloud GmbH | Hosting and storage | Frankfurt, Germany |
| Tailspin Search Inc. | Search indexing | Dublin, Ireland |
| Litware Mail Ltd. | Transactional email | London, United Kingdom |

## 4. Data Subject Requests

The Processor will promptly forward any request from a data subject to the Controller and will not respond directly unless instructed. The Processor will assist the Controller in responding within the statutory deadlines, at no additional charge for up to ten (10) requests per month.

## 5. International Transfers

Personal Data will be stored in the European Economic Area. Any transfer outside it requires the Controller's prior written consent and must rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission.

## 6. Audits

The Controller may audit the Processor's compliance once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' written notice. The Processor may instead provide a current ISO/IEC 27001 certificate and its most recent SOC 2 Type II report.

## 7. Return and Deletion

On termination of the Agreement, the Processor will, at the Controller's choice, return or delete all Personal Data within sixty (60) days, unless retention is required by law, and will certify the deletion in writing.
