A note about this place

An imagined brand. A complete design experience.

A fictional concept

Marée, its venue, programmes, address, availability, and prices are invented for a design portfolio. The site is not an offer from an operating business.

No real bookings

All planning and enquiry journeys are demonstrations. No reservation is created, no payment is requested, and no confirmation email is sent. Completing a form does not create a contract.

Reference imagery

Stock photographs illustrate the intended atmosphere. People pictured are not represented as staff, customers, or endorsers. Images do not depict an actual venue belonging to this fictional brand.

Responsible reuse

The application source is available under the repository’s MIT License. Photography and fonts retain their own licences, listed in Credits and the bundled font licence files. Replace fictional business information and obtain appropriate professional review before adapting this concept for a real venue.
