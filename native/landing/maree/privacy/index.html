The details matter

Your privacy

A quiet, straightforward approach to your information.

This is a demonstration

Marée is a fictional marketing-site concept. The visit and group forms run locally in your browser tab. They do not send enquiries, create reservations, process payments, or store the information you enter.

Your inputs

Form inputs are held temporarily in browser memory and are lost when you reload or leave the page. A downloaded sample plan stays on your own device. Do not enter sensitive information into a demonstration.

Site requests

This project does not add analytics, advertising trackers, third-party font requests, or application cookies. Images and fonts are served locally. The hosting service may process ordinary request logs; review its policy before deploying a live service.

Before real use

A real operator would need an appropriate privacy notice, verified contact details, retention rules, and a secure processing system. This page describes the demo as built, not a production business.
