The details matter

A welcome for everyone

Thoughtful interaction is part of the design.

Navigating the website

The site uses semantic headings, labelled controls, native form validation, keyboard-visible focus, and a skip-to-content link. The mobile menu uses a shadcn Sheet powered by Base UI with Escape support and focus management.

A calmer experience

When your device requests reduced motion, decorative animations, scroll reveals, and animated transitions stop. Essential content remains visible. Interactive controls do not rely on animation to explain their state.

Readable and responsive

Layouts adapt to small screens. Images have alternative text where they convey meaning. Form prices and search results expose live updates to assistive technology. Colour is accompanied by labels for selected options and routes.

Scope

These features are design intentions and implementation details, not a certification of WCAG compliance. The physical venue is fictional, so on-site accessibility cannot be verified. A complete assistive-technology audit should precede a real launch.
