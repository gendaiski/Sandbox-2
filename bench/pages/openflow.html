What the test does

Builds a probe linked against OpenFlow's own engine crate and validates the example workflows and a contract-review workflow, including its parallel layers. Broken workflows (cycles, self-edges, dangling edges, duplicates, bad schemas, empty) must be rejected. Runs the engine unit tests and the orchestration headless end-to-end suite.

The orchestration suite needs libdbus-1-dev and pkg-config on Linux.

Findings

  • Non-object output schemas fall back to a default instead of failing.
