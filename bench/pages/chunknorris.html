What the test does

Parses the sample services agreement as Markdown, HTML and PDF and chunks each one. Checks that parent headers are carried into every chunk, that the word and token caps split oversized sections, that chunk_string works on in-memory text, the JSON export, and page numbers on PDF chunks.

The token-cap check downloads the tiktoken vocabulary on first run.
