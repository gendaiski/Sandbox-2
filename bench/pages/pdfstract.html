What the test does

Extracts the sample PDF with pymupdf4llm and markitdown (markdown, text and JSON), runs the token, sentence, recursive and semantic chunkers, embeds with sentence-transformers, and tests convert_chunk, batch_convert, the pdfstract CLI and the FastAPI web backend.

setup.sh standard or advanced installs the OCR and ML extractors.
