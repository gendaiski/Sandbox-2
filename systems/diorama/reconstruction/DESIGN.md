# Diorama showcase reconstructions

Repair of the rejected preview. Retain the explicitly requested original pixel style, three-quarter hero camera, white paper background, free orbit and minimal scene controls. Use original MIT toolkit rigs/poses/post-processing and new geometry derived from the supplied films. This is reconstruction work, not recovery of the author's missing scene files. No acquired external model is represented as original; there are no external model dependencies.

SceneProfile narrative; uiMode minimal-controls. Existing repair keeps original visual choices. Four independent scene roots, deterministic functions of time. Scene switches dispose geometry/materials/texture resources and preserve shared renderer. Loops: Pool 26s, Bank Job 48s, Dust Kings 38s, creature battle 46s. Mandatory visible inventories are recorded in manifest per scene, not substituted by effects harnesses.

Pool: tiled deck/coping/basin, turquoise water, ladders, diving board/supports, lifebuoy, beach ball, towel, articulated swimmer, diver, lifeguard, sunbather and edge sitter. Swimming, dive/splash/resurface/return, paced patrol and water motion.
Bank: three detailed facades, bank columns/steps/entrance, roof signage/vents, striped awning, street lights/bench/hydrant/bins/manhole, sedan, two police cars, four robbers and four officers. Arrival, entrance, alarm, departure and police response.
Dust Kings: garage, grandstand/crowd, rails/barriers, sponsor signage, flags, dirt/tyre tracks, ramp and cones, yellow/red monster trucks, display sequence and fire. Driven paths, wheel rotation, jump/suspension, crowd and flags.
Battle: walled courtyard, stone arch/panels, paving, trees/rocks/torches, two trainers, authored recognisable yellow mouse and orange winged dragon rigs, ball, lightning/fire/recall effects. Arrival, attack anticipation/contact/recovery, defeat and recall.

Acceptance: actual WebGL scene output, no video as canvas fallback; multi-frame capture each scene; freeze repeatability; hero and mobile fit; orbit/reset, timeline, speed and downloads; console/finite-transform/resource checks. Visual discrepancies remain documented rather than certified as pixel-identical. Reference film available explicitly as a separate comparison overlay.
