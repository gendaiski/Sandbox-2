    Your first battle

    Choose Pikachu or Charizard and press Start battle. You always act first, then the local opponent responds.

    1. Use your special attack for higher damage. You have six uses (PP).
    2. The physical attack has unlimited uses.
    3. Every third turn the opponent prepares a stronger attack. Guard reduces that turn’s damage by 70%.
    4. Use one of two potions to restore up to 36 HP. The opponent still attacks after you heal.

    Reduce the opponent to zero HP to win. Electric attacks get a 1.25× bonus against Charizard. These are simplified game rules, not a simulation of the official games. New battle resets the fight. Battles end in a draw after 60 turns.

    Wins and losses are saved only on this browser. No account, API key or internet connection is needed for gameplay.
